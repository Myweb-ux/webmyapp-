    <h2>What Are HTML Headings?</h2>

    <p>
        HTML headings are used to organize and structure the
        content of a webpage.
    </p>

    <p>
        HTML provides six heading levels, from
        <code>&lt;h1&gt;</code> to <code>&lt;h6&gt;</code>.
    </p>


    <h2>The Six Heading Levels</h2>

    <pre><code>&lt;h1&gt;Heading 1&lt;/h1&gt;

&lt;h2&gt;Heading 2&lt;/h2&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;Heading 3&lt;/h3&gt;

&lt;h4&gt;Heading 4&lt;/h4&gt;

&lt;h5&gt;Heading 5&lt;/h5&gt;

&lt;h6&gt;Heading 6&lt;/h6&gt;</code></pre>


    <h2>Heading 1</h2>

    <p>
        <code>&lt;h1&gt;</code> is normally used for the main
        heading of a page.
    </p>

    <pre><code>&lt;h1&gt;Web Development&lt;/h1&gt;</code></pre>


    <h2>Heading 2</h2>

    <p>
        <code>&lt;h2&gt;</code> is commonly used for major sections
        within a webpage.
    </p>

    <pre><code>&lt;h2&gt;HTML Basics&lt;/h2&gt;</code></pre>


    <h2>Heading 3</h2>

    <p>
        <code>&lt;h3&gt;</code> can be used for subsections inside
        an <code>&lt;h2&gt;</code> section.
    </p>

    <pre><code>&lt;h3&gt;HTML Elements&lt;/h3&gt;</code></pre>


    <h2>Why Heading Structure Matters</h2>

    <p>
        Good heading structure makes your content easier to read
        and understand.
    </p>

    <p>
        It also helps search engines and assistive technologies
        understand the organization of your page.
    </p>


    <div class="alert alert-info">

        <strong>Tip:</strong>

        Think of headings like the chapters and sections
        of a book.

    </div>


    <h2>Example</h2>

    <pre><code>&lt;h1&gt;My Website&lt;/h1&gt;

&lt;h2&gt;About Me&lt;/h2&gt;

&lt;p&gt;Welcome to my website.&lt;/p&gt;

&lt;h2&gt;My Projects&lt;/h2&gt;

&lt;h3&gt;Project One&lt;/h3&gt;

&lt;p&gt;This is my first project.&lt;/p&gt;</code></pre>


    <h2>Quick Exercise</h2>

    <p>
        Create a webpage with:
    </p>

    <ol>

        <li>One <code>&lt;h1&gt;</code></li>

        <li>Two <code>&lt;h2&gt;</code> headings</li>

        <li>One <code>&lt;h3&gt;</code> under each section</li>

    </ol>


    <div class="alert alert-success">

        <strong>Remember:</strong>

        Use headings to organize content logically,
        not simply to make text appear larger.

    </div>


    <h2>Summary</h2>

    <p>
        HTML provides six heading levels:
        <code>&lt;h1&gt;</code> through <code>&lt;h6&gt;</code>.
        They help organize webpage content into a clear hierarchy.
    </p>
